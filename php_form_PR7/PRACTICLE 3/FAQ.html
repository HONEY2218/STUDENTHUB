📖 Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions asked by students regarding StudentHub Portal.

Questions & Answers

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is an online portal that helps students manage academic information, results, fees, events and profiles.

2. How can I register on StudentHub?

Go to the Register page, fill in the registration form with valid details and click the Register button.

3. How do I log in?

Enter your registered Email ID and Password on the Login page and click Login.

4. Can I update my profile?

Yes. Visit the Profile page and update your personal or academic information.

5. Where can I check my semester result?

Open the Result page from the navigation menu to view your semester examination results.

6. How can I check my fee details?

Visit the Fees page to view your fee payment status and receipt information.

7. Can I use StudentHub on my mobile phone?

Yes. StudentHub is fully responsive and works on desktops, tablets and smartphones.

8. How do I reset my password?

Click on the Forgot Password option on the Login page and follow the instructions.

9. Where can I see upcoming events?

Open the Events page to view workshops, seminars, hackathons and other campus activities.

10. Which browser is recommended?

Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge and Mozilla Firefox are recommended for the best experience.

11. Is StudentHub available 24×7?

Yes. StudentHub services are available 24 hours a day with an internet connection.
